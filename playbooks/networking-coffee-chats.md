# Playbook: networking and coffee chats (the original use case)

**Goal:** short calls with people who can help you: recruiters, alumni, people a few years ahead on your path. It works for students, career changers and anyone breaking into a field.

- **Who:** at large companies, the campus or early-talent recruiter. At startups and small teams, the person who'd manage you. Strong ties first: same school, same club, same former employer, same city.
- **How many:** 2–3 people per company at most, each with a different message.
- **Connection note:** for the highest-value person at each company. It names the shared tie or one specific thing from their profile or posts. Everyone else gets a bare request, then a message within 2 days of accepting.
- **First message:** thanks for connecting, one true line about you (a real moment, not a list of skills), one question about their path, and a 15-minute call with a loose time window.
- **Never:** ask for a job, attach a resume unasked, or send the same text to two people at one company.
- **After the call:** a thank-you within 24 hours with one real detail from the conversation, and log it as `meeting`.

Example shape:
> Hi Jordan, thanks for connecting! I'm a second-year marketing student, and running our club's sponsor drive is what got me curious about partnerships. I'd love to hear how you went from agency work to leading partnerships for a pro team. Open to a quick 15-minute call sometime in the next couple of weeks?
